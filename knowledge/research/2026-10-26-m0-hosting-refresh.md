## Additional caveats captured by this refresh

These clarify the current documents; without archived earlier page bytes, they are not labelled as provider changes made after October 23.

1. **Fly trial auto-stop:** trial machines are documented to stop automatically after five minutes. This is distinct from the two-hour/seven-day total trial allowance. [Fly trial](https://docs.fly.io/about/free-trial).
2. **Railway sleep timing:** optional Serverless detects outbound inactivity by sampling, so actual sleep is documented as five to ten minutes after the last outbound traffic. Applying Serverless requires redeployment; active DB pools can prevent sleep. A wake request can receive 502. This is not the approved game service-time clock. [Serverless](https://docs.railway.com/deployments/serverless).
3. **Neon region-specific retention:** the region page states Azure regions are deprecated, new Azure projects cannot be created, and Azure Free projects inactive for at least 90 days are subject to deletion as of October 5, 2026. This is a separate policy from quota exhaustion not deleting data. The Singapore candidate is AWS; no user's existing region was inspected. [Neon regions](https://neon.com/docs/introduction/regions).
4. **Railway control boundaries remain separate:** 10,000 platform network connections are not a Free CPU/RAM game-capacity guarantee. A Compute hard usage limit takes workloads offline; an email threshold does not stop them. No such limit was configured here. [Network limits](https://docs.railway.com/networking/public-networking/specs-and-limits), [cost control](https://docs.railway.com/pricing/cost-control).
