# M0.4 — Free hosting and durable storage candidates

Investigation started 2026-10-22; official pricing/free-plan pages refreshed 2026-10-23 (Asia/Saigon). This is a document-based comparison for [M0.4 — Prototype online và lưu/khôi phục trên free tier](https://github.com/chuongphong12/Sanguosha-vn/issues/30), following the approved [session contract](2026-10-22-m0-session-contract.md). No account, dashboard, billing instrument, provider environment or live service was inspected or created. No provider latency, capacity, cold start or recovery was measured.

## Recommendation to test, not a vendor selection

Later document access and additional operational caveats are recorded in the [2026-10-26 refresh](2026-10-26-m0-hosting-refresh.md). This original investigation retains its October 23 access date and evidence; neither note selects or provisions a provider.

**First candidate:** one Render Free Node service plus a separate Neon Free Postgres project, preferably both in Singapore. Keep the existing TypeScript/PixiJS/boardgame.io stack and application-owned sequencing/staged Master output. This combination is an engineering hypothesis: the documents establish available components, not that the full five-room/bot workload fits or that the session implementation satisfies the contract. Supabase Free Postgres is an alternative if its pause and connectivity behavior prove acceptable. No provider is selected by this note.

Finish the local real-transport/storage experiment first. A scratch local file proves only that local experiment; it cannot certify durable recovery on an ephemeral provider filesystem. Provider execution needs separately authorized access and a genuine external-store adapter. Keep the remote portion of M0.4 open until those traces execute on the selected service.

Neon's October 2, 2026 announcement and current plan/pricing pages agree on **1 GB/project**. An older official repository FAQ still returned 0.5 GB in search; this note uses the current plan pages, not that stale figure. [Dated announcement](https://neon.com/blog/neon-free-plan-1-gb-per-project).

Neon's PgBouncer can accept up to 10,000 client connections; active Postgres transactions/connections remain constrained by compute and pool size. Its transaction mode does not preserve session-level advisory locks, LISTEN/NOTIFY or arbitrary session state. [Pool semantics](https://neon.com/docs/connect/connection-pooling). Therefore the proposed writer lease/revision check must be durable transaction data with a commit-time fence, rather than an assumed persistent pooled connection. The 10,000 limit is not a bot/game benchmark.

Supabase's published **200 Realtime peak connections** concern its Realtime product, not Node Socket.IO sockets or a guarantee of 200 direct Postgres connections. This proof does not require Supabase Realtime or user accounts. [Billing units](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/billing-on-supabase). Pool and database connection limits still require checking against the actual chosen project and workload.

## Ownership and recovery remain application work

These are implications of the approved contract, not capabilities supplied automatically by a free plan:

1. Keep room generation/revision, credentials, controller generation, accepted state/RNG/continuation, idempotency receipt and pending recipient output inside one accepted transaction boundary. Publish ACK/views only after commit; a provider database adapter under stock Master does not change its output ordering.
2. Fence old writers and bot jobs at commit, including during deployment overlap or reconnect to a new process. A single replica or an in-process queue does not prove absence of an older process or serialize concurrent lobby/presence paths.
3. Persist approved remaining decision/grace time and establish recovery readiness before restarting service-time clocks. Neither host sleep nor DB suspension is explicit human Leave.
4. Execute last-online-human Leave deletion even when another actor has grace; revoke credentials/work and remove private live state. Reject stale sync/join/write attempts after deletion. No provider idle/pause policy is a substitute for this application deletion policy.
5. If multiple Socket.IO nodes are later introduced, configure polling affinity or a deliberate WebSocket-only choice, plus inter-node message routing. Those solve transport routing, not durable room-writer ownership. [Socket.IO multiple-node requirements](https://socket.io/docs/v4/using-multiple-nodes/).
