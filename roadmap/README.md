# Delivery roadmap

## Engine

See the [current Hegemony port plan](../plans/hegemony-port.md) for completed library boundaries and remaining production gates. Parts 4y/4z provide the [SkillCard envelope](../engine/skill-card-on-use.md) and [shared GameRule/resources/effects](../engine/skill-card-execution.md). Part 4aa adds the [normal Room wrapper and authoritative Play/targetful response-use gateway](../engine/room-use-and-admission.md). Parts 4ab.1/4ab.2 add [MethodUse/MethodResponse CardAsked/provide/acquisition/retry and distinct execution](../engine/direct-card-use.md). Part 4ab.3 adds [None/Discard and normalized retrial/Guicai/Guidao composition](../engine/retrial.md). Part 4ab.4 adds [actual SinglePeach/Room rescue composition](../engine/single-peach.md). Part 4ac adds [Room judgment](../engine/judgment.md) and [draw/recycling/reservations](../engine/draw-pile.md). Part 4ad adds [batch/player drawing](../engine/card-drawing.md) and [Draw-phase orchestration](../engine/draw-phase.md) with real free-chain composition. Part 4ae adds [global GameStart/initial dealing](../engine/game-start.md) and [luck-card state/manual transfers](../engine/luck-cards.md). Next scope remaining phase/turn and complete startup/package orchestration, then unwind and live package/application integration. These checkpoints do not enable production SkillCards.
